1. KISS (Keep It Simple and Stupid)
    - based on the fact that most of the system works best if they are kept simple rather than make it complex

Why?
    - less code, takes less time to write, debug and understand
    - Easy to modify.
    - Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.
    - it seems that perfection is reached not when there is nothing left to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.


2. YAGNI (You aren't Gonna Need It)
    - it means dont implement something until it is neccesary.
    - Always implement things when you need them, and never when you just foresee that you need them.
